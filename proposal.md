## 1. Executive Summary
Most outpatient clinics lose **15% to 25% of potential consultations** due to missed calls after clinic hours, busy front desks during peak morning rounds, and unrecovered appointment cancellations.

## 3. How It Works (Zero Workflow Disruption)
1. **Patient Messages or Calls**: Patient texts your clinic WhatsApp or calls your front-desk line.
2. **AI Triages & Confirms**: System checks real-time doctor availability, respects clinic hours/leaves, and confirms the booking.
3. **Live Sync with Staff**: Front desk monitors everything in real time via a web dashboard; staff can take over any chat with a single click.
4. **Doctor Command Center**: Doctors receive automated morning briefings on WhatsApp and can block dates or unblock days directly via text.
